Who RentManor is for

Small portfolios. Trusted people. Not an office full of property managers.

For you if

  • You manage a few rentals on your phone
  • You share a portfolio with family, a partner, or a helper
  • You want each investor’s properties kept separate
  • You want something clear and usable on a phone

Not for

  • Large property-management companies
  • Payments, maintenance tickets, or maps-as-a-product
  • App Store installs (this is Add to Home Screen from Safari)
