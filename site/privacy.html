Privacy

Updated: September 19, 2026

RentManor stores the rental records you choose to keep in your account.

What we store

  • Your account email
  • Property and tenant details you enter (names, phones, emails, notes, vacancy)
  • Invite records when you invite someone to your account (their email and membership in that account)

Who can see what

  • Only members of an account can see that account’s properties and tenants
  • An invitee you add can see and edit the same portfolio
  • Other people’s accounts stay separate — we don’t mix portfolios

Leaving / export

  • Sign out anytime
  • Use Backup in the app to export a JSON copy of your account’s properties
  • To delete an account or ask that data be removed, contact the person who runs this RentManor site

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your information. We don’t run ads against your property list.

Short product notice — not legal advice.
