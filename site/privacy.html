Privacy

Updated: September 19, 2026

RentManor helps you manage rental property records for your account.

What we store

Account email, and the property and tenant information you enter. Data for an account stays with that account. Invited members of an account can see that account’s properties.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your information. We don’t run ads on your property list. We don’t mix one investor’s portfolio into another’s.

Access

You can sign out anytime. You can export a backup of your account data from the app. If you want an account deleted, contact the person who runs this RentManor deployment.

Contact

Questions about this product’s privacy: ask the site operator (Damon for this deployment).
