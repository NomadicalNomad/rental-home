Privacy

Draft updated: October 6, 2026 — owner approval pending

RentManor helps you manage rental property records for your account.

What we store

Account email, and the property and tenant information you enter. Data for an account stays with that account. Owners and invited members can view, edit, delete and completely replace account records. Only owners administer invitations.

What we don’t do

The implementation scopes private records to account memberships. Data-use and retention policies require the operator’s published policy.

Access

You can sign out anytime. You can export a backup of your account data from the app. If you want an account deleted, contact the person who runs this RentManor deployment.

Contact

Questions about this product’s privacy: ask the site operator (Damon for this deployment).
