How RentManor works

The basics — then optional Home Screen install.

Create an account

Sign up with email and password. That’s your private portfolio.

Add your rentals

Properties and tenants live in your account. Search the list. Open a detail. Edit when something changes.

Stay in touch

Call, text, or email a tenant from the property screen when a number or address is on file.

Invite when you share

Need a partner or helper on the same properties? Invite them into your account after you sign in. They get their own login into your account. Other investors keep separate accounts — no mixing. Don’t share your password; use invite.

Optional

Backup / restore

Export or import a JSON backup into the signed-in account when you need a spare copy.

Add RentManor to your iPhone

Takes about a minute. Use Safari — not Chrome.

  1. Open the app in Safari (not Chrome): https://nomadicalnomad.github.io/rental-home/app/ (When app.rentmanor.com is live, use that instead.)
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

After that, the RentManor icon sits on your home screen. Open it anytime — you’ll sign in like usual.

On a computer, bookmark the site. Home Screen install is for iPhone Safari.
